Loan Adjudication: Project Scope

Build and evaluate a system that converts a synthetic personal-loan conversation and supplied policy rules into structured application fields, a decision, failed rule IDs, and an explanation.

The project includes reproducible synthetic-data generation, preprocessing with the model's chat template, QLoRA fine-tuning, and generation-mode evaluation. Evaluation covers standard unseen conversations, adversarial dialogue, and changed policy rules.

A deterministic rules engine recomputes the final decision from extracted fields. The model's own decision is retained for comparison. Missing or unresolved evidence must remain explicit.

Artifacts include training and evaluation scripts, a model adapter, synthetic datasets, metrics, a technical report, and an interactive walkthrough. This is a synthetic-data research project, not a validated lending system.
